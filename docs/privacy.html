Skylance privacy policy

Skylance asks for no name, email, photo, contacts or location. This page lists the little data the game does use, why, and how long it is kept.

Effective 25 November 2026. Applies to the Skylance app for Android (com.skylance.game) and the web version.

Ads

Videos are optional. A player can choose to watch one in the Hangar for free gems, up to 5 times a day. No ads appear during play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

  • Players under 13: every ad request is marked as directed to a child, rated G, and never personalised. Google does not use these requests to build an advertising profile.
  • Players 13 and over: AdMob may use the device's advertising ID and general device information to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first, and players under 16 there are treated as under the age of consent.

Read how Google uses data in apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings, under Google, then Ads.

Purchases

Gem packs are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, so we never see card or bank details. After a purchase, the game sends the purchase token to our server, which asks Google to confirm it before the gems are given. Parents can require approval for purchases with Google Play's parental controls and Family Link.

Friend invites and challenge links

An invite or challenge link contains your random player code and, for challenges, a level and score. When someone installs the game from your link, Google Play passes that code to the app so both players get their reward. Links contain nothing that identifies you personally.

Children

Skylance is made for everyone, including children. On first launch the app asks for a year of birth and keeps only the age group. For children under 13, the app shows only child-directed, non-personalised ads and collects nothing beyond the table above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent who believes their child's data should be removed can contact us as described below.

Sharing

We do not sell data. Data is shared only with the services that run the game: Supabase (our database host, servers in Frankfurt, Germany), Google Play (installs, purchases and invite codes) and Google AdMob (ads, as described above).

Your choices and deleting data

  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device.
  • To delete the records on our server, send us your player code (the last six characters of any invite link you shared). We will delete them within 30 days. Purchase records needed for refunds or tax law are kept only as long as the law requires.
  • You can change ad consent at any time by reinstalling the app, and reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
Contact. Use the developer email address shown on the Skylance page in the Google Play Store.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Our database tables cannot be read by the public, and only three narrow functions can be called by the app, each checking the player's secret. Network addresses are stored only as one-way hashes and deleted after 2 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. Significant changes are also announced in the app.
